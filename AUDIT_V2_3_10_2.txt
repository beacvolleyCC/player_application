PLAYER V2.3.10.2 — HIDDEN SELF TEST TRIPLE TAP

Scope:
- Replaces the iOS-unfriendly long-press QA unlock.
- Adds a fixed, non-text status-dot hit target next to "Telefonos értesítések".
- Three taps on that dot within the short gesture window reveal the existing self-test row.
- QA reveal remains available only while the current device push state is active.
- Existing self-test RPC, inbox logic, push sender, preferences, profile, RSVP and notification behavior are unchanged.
- No SQL migration.

iOS UX rationale:
- No long press -> no Safari/iOS text-selection or touch-callout behavior.
- The hit target is a button surface with touch-action: manipulation and user-select/touch-callout disabled.
- The visible title/status text remains normal and does not move during the gesture.
