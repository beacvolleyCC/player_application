PLAYER V2.3.10.9 AUDIT

Scope
- Notification swipe interaction only, plus build/cache version bump.
- Built on V2.3.10.8, which already contains the V2.3.10.7 Player UI consistency changes.

Gesture design
- Full-size rounded red action layer under the entire notification card.
- Action layer hidden at rest; revealed only by the card moving left over it.
- No finite 112px red strip in the effective final rule.
- No progressive action opacity animation.
- No row-height collapse animation after dismissal.
- Pointer capture starts only after horizontal intent is established.
- Forgiving horizontal direction lock for diagonal thumb movement.
- Card follows horizontal finger movement directly and is clamped to its own width.
- Distance threshold: 23% of card width, clamped to 66–92px.
- Flick: minimum 22px plus leftward velocity <= -0.38 px/ms.
- Snap-back: 155ms.
- Dismiss exit: 165ms.
- Optional light haptic via navigator.vibrate where supported.
- Dismiss re-renders synchronously at the end of card exit, preventing a red-only tail.
